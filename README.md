#  Shift HR

A modern, streamlined **Human Resource & Shift Management System** designed to simplify employee scheduling, time tracking, leave requests, and workforce management.

---

##  Features

-  **Shift Scheduling & Management**: Create, assign, and publish recurring or dynamic shifts with automatic conflict detection.
-  **Employee Directory**: Centralized hub for managing employee profiles, roles, departments, and contact information.
-  **Attendance & Time Tracking**: Clock-in/clock-out tracking, shift swaps, and overtime calculations.
-  **Leave & PTO Tracking**: Easy submission and approval workflow for sick leave, vacations, and personal time off.
-  **Analytics & Reporting**: Visual dashboards for shift coverage, attendance rates, and labor cost distribution.
-  **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**: Custom permission levels for **Admin**, **HR Manager**, **Supervisor**, and **Employee**.
